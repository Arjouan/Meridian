# Market study

An evidence-based look at the market this project targets: its size, why now, who it's for, the
competition, what makes our solution unique, and the impact it could have.

> **Methodology & honesty note:** figures below come from secondary market-research and official EU
> sources (listed at the end). Analyst market-size numbers vary *widely* by definition and firm, so
> they are shown as ranges and treated as directional, not precise. Validate before using in a pitch.

---

## 1. Executive summary

Maritime logistics is a large, fast-digitalising market ($10B+ in relevant software segments,
growing ~10–14%/yr) under a **wave of new carbon regulation** that forces operators to measure and
report emissions. The big visibility platforms (project44, FourKites, e2open…) chase large shippers
and are closed and expensive. **Small and regional carriers — the majority of operators by count —
are underserved.** Our wedge: an **open, affordable, emissions-first** visibility tool for exactly
those operators. The regulatory deadlines make emissions a *painkiller* (a mandatory purchase), not
a *vitamin*, which is what makes the opportunity real rather than merely nice.

## 2. Market size & growth

| Segment | Size (recent) | Growth (CAGR) |
|---|---|---|
| Shipping / maritime software (broad) | ~$2B–$17B in 2025 depending on definition [1][2] | ~10–13% [1][2] |
| Container visibility platforms | ~$8.2B (2025) → ~$32.6B (2034), one estimate [3] | ~14% [3] |

**Takeaway:** even the *conservative* readings describe a multi-billion-dollar, double-digit-growth
space. We don't need a large share — a tiny slice of an underserved niche is a real business.

## 3. Why now — the drivers

**A. Carbon regulation (the decisive one).** A stack of rules now *requires* emissions measurement
and reporting from ships calling at EU ports:
- **EU ETS extended to shipping from Jan 2024** — companies surrender allowances for **40% of 2024
  emissions, 70% of 2025, 100% from 2026** [4][5]. This puts a real price on carbon.
- **FuelEU Maritime** — fully applies from **1 Jan 2025**; first reporting year is 2025 [6].
- **IMO EEXI + CII** ratings and the EU **MRV** reporting regime already oblige monitoring [4].

This is the difference-maker: compliance is **mandatory, deadline-driven, and budgeted** — the
classic conditions under which software actually gets bought.

**B. Digitalisation & visibility demand.** Operators are replacing spreadsheets/email with software
to cut delays, avoid cargo loss, and improve customer service — the reason the visibility market is
growing double digits [1][3].

## 4. Target segment — who it's for

**Small and regional carriers, short-sea & feeder operators, and ship managers.** Evidence this is
real and underserved:
- **~2,200 vessels** operate in intra-European short-sea shipping [7][8].
- The market is **highly fragmented: >50% of vessels belong to 1–5 ship fleets** [8]. These operators
  can't afford (or justify) enterprise suites and often run on spreadsheets and email.
- Concentrated geographies to start: **Italy, the Netherlands and Spain account for >40%** of EU
  short-sea tonnage [7] — a focused beachhead.

These operators feel the new regulation acutely but lack affordable tooling to comply — precisely
our opening.

## 5. Competitive landscape

| Player type | Examples | Focus | Gap we exploit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enterprise visibility | project44, FourKites, e2open [3] | large shippers/3PLs; broad SCV | expensive, closed, overkill for small carriers |
| Ocean/container specialists | CargoSmart, OpenTrack, Blume Global [3] | big carriers/forwarders | not tailored to small operators; limited emissions focus |
| Forwarder platforms | Flexport, BuyCo [3] | forwarding workflow | not an operator's own ops+compliance tool |

**The gap:** none combine **(a) small/regional-carrier focus, (b) open-source & self-hostable,
(c) modern real-time UX, and (d) built-in emissions/compliance** in one affordable package. That
four-way combination is our defensible position.

## 6. What makes our solution unique & useful

1. **Emissions-first & compliance-native** — CO₂ per voyage/container and regulator-ready reports,
   turning a legal obligation into a one-click output. (Most affordable tools bolt this on, if at all.)
2. **Open-source & self-hostable** — trust, no lock-in, and a free on-ramp; rare in this space and a
   strong adoption lever for cautious small operators.
3. **Focused on the underserved** — built for 1–5-ship operators, not Maersk. Right features, right
   price, fast onboarding.
4. **Modern, map-first, real-time UX** — the antithesis of the dated software this industry runs on;
   an instant credibility signal.

## 7. Impact the solution could have

**For the operator (economic):**
- Cuts manual tracking/admin (spreadsheets, email chasing) → time saved.
- Reduces **demurrage & detention** and missed ETAs through live visibility.
- Slashes **compliance admin and fine/penalty risk** under EU ETS/FuelEU by automating measurement
  and reporting.
- Better vessel/container utilisation from clearer operational data.

**Environmental / societal:**
- "You can't reduce what you don't measure" — per-voyage CO₂ transparency enables actual reduction
  and better **CII** ratings, directly supporting EU/IMO decarbonisation goals [4][6].
- **Levels the playing field**: gives small operators the compliance and visibility tools that today
  only large lines can afford — keeping smaller, often greener short-sea operators competitive.

**For you (the builder):** a portfolio piece on a real, regulated, high-impact problem — strong for
hiring even in the scenario where the product itself stays small.

## 8. TAM / SAM / SOM (illustrative)

Rough, directional — refine with primary research:
- **TAM** — global maritime-ops + container-visibility software: **tens of $B** and growing [1][3].
- **SAM** — visibility + emissions-compliance software for **small/regional carriers in Europe**:
  a low-single-digit-% slice of TAM (hundreds of $M range).
- **SOM (realistic early)** — a few dozen to a few hundred small operators on a modest per-vessel or
  per-tenant subscription. One design partner → a handful of paying operators is the first milestone.

## 9. Risks & challenges (honest)

- **Long, trust-heavy sales cycles**; small operators are cautious and slow to adopt.
- **Data-quality/integration** (AIS, EDI, port systems) is real work; start with CSV import.
- **Incumbents could move down-market** — our open-source + niche focus is the moat.
- **Solo-founder credibility** — mitigated by open-source, self-hosting, and a reference design partner.
- **Regulatory complexity** — an opportunity (drives demand) but requires keeping the compliance
  logic accurate and current.

## 10. Go-to-market (recap)

Lead with the **painkiller**: the emissions/compliance report operators must produce anyway. Land
**one design partner** among small/regional carriers, turn them into a reference, expand into
tracking/ops, and grow via open-source adoption + content. (Full detail in `Launch-Plan.docx`.)

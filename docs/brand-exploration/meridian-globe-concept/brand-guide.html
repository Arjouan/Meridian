VMS-blue design language

Brand Guide

The Meridian name paired with the navy + blue, line-art design you liked from VMS. The mark is a globe crossed by a meridian - navigation and precision - with a single blue waypoint on the route.

01  Name & tagline

Meridian - a line of longitude used to navigate the globe, and figuratively a point of reference and highest quality. It signals precision, global reach, and authority.

Tagline (pick one):
• "The reference line for your fleet."
• "Navigate global trade with confidence."
• "Precision visibility and emissions for maritime logistics."

02  Logo

Line-art, never on a coloured tile. A navy globe with a blue meridian and a blue waypoint. On dark, the navy turns white and the accents brighten to cyan.

Files: meridian-logo-full.svg, meridian-logo-icon.svg.

05  Text colours & tone

Text on backgrounds
  • On light: body #0F172A, secondary #64748B
  • On navy: white, dim white at 80%
  • Links & actions: Blue #1E88E5 (deep #1565C0 on light)
  • Data/IDs always in mono
Tone of voice
  • Precise - exact figures, no vagueness
  • Composed - calm authority
  • Global - speaks to worldwide trade
  • Professional - enterprise-credible

Inspired by AMS / AIR22 — blue variant

Brand Guide

Visual identity for VMS - Vessel Management Systems, blue variant. Same AMS heritage - deep navy, a twin-stroke swoosh, a bold acronym over a wide-tracked tagline - with the accent shifted from teal to blue, echoing the sister AIR22 mark.

Lineage: this is the blue version of the AMS-inspired VMS mark. The navy sea swell and hull are unchanged; the swoosh trail is now blue #1E88E5 rather than teal, bringing it closer to AIR22's blue-and-cyan family.

01  Name & tagline

A direct maritime parallel to AMS.

VMS - Vessel Management Systems. A three-letter acronym in the AMS mould, immediately readable as serious operational software. Broad enough to cover tracking, maintenance, and emissions.

Tagline (pick one):
• "The operating system for your fleet."
• "Vessels, cargo, and compliance - in one system."
• "Maritime operations, brought together."

Variants if you prefer: MMS (Maritime Management Systems), FMS (Fleet Management Systems).

02  Logo

Line-art, never on a coloured tile (just like AMS). A navy sea swell with a blue swoosh trail, and a navy hull beneath. On dark backgrounds the navy strokes turn white; the blue stays.

Files: vms-logo-full.svg, vms-logo-icon.svg. On dark backgrounds the swoosh brightens to cyan #35C2F0 for contrast.

05  Text colours & tone

Text on backgrounds
  • On light: body #0F172A, secondary #64748B
  • On navy: white, dim white at 80%
  • Links & actions: Blue #1E88E5 (deep #1565C0 on light)
  • Data/IDs always in mono
Tone of voice
  • Operational - speaks the language of fleet managers
  • Dependable - calm, systematic, exact
  • Clear - plain words, real numbers and units
  • Professional - enterprise-credible, never flippant
